**No login.** You don't sign in to Steam, type a password or set up an API key. A public profile link is enough.

**Easiest first.** Missing achievements are sorted by how many players already have them, so the quick wins come before the 0.5% grinds.

**Real steps, not a wall of wiki text.** Click *How to get it* for a short numbered walkthrough, a "missable" warning when it matters, a rough time estimate, and links to Steam guides and YouTube.

**You pick the game.** No background scan of your entire backlog. What you open is saved on your PC and only refreshed after you've played again.

**10 languages.** English, 简体中文, Русский, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 한국어, Polski.

**Updates in one click.** When a new version lands, the app shows a button. Click it, done.

## Install

1. Download `AchievementHunter-Setup-<version>.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/YoungRedfox/achievement-hunter-releases/releases/latest).
2. Run it. Windows may show a SmartScreen notice while the app builds its reputation: choose *More info → Run anyway*.
3. Paste your Steam profile link. Make sure your profile and *Game details* are set to public.

## Privacy, in plain words

Your profile, keys and saved data stay on your PC, encrypted by Windows. We don't upload them. The app's server only passes along public Steam requests and writes guides from a game and achievement number. No accounts, no logs.
